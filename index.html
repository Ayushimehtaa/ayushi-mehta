Featured analysis

The region that sold the most was not the one that kept the most.

An Excel analysis of e-commerce sales, customer segments and order outcomes.

  • Revenue and profit disagree. The South led in net sales at $55.59M, while the North had the highest margin at 46.10%.
  • Loyal customers carry the business. They were the top segment at $166.18M in net sales.
  • Completed orders do the work. $144.20M of $177.13M total net sales, at a 42.99% margin.
  • Next step. Look into regional margin drivers, cancellations and returns.
